About Me

Hey! I’m really interested in robotics, AI, and neuroscience, so I figured I’d make a website to share what I’m working on! I’ll be posting what I'm learning and whatever else I end up experimenting with along the way. I think it’s pretty cool how coding can connect to so many different things, especially how the brain works and how we can build machines that do things we never thought possible. I’m still figuring a lot of things out, so there will probably be some trial and error (and a lot of Googling). I made this site to keep track of my progress, share my projects on GitHub, and hopefully come up with some new ideas along the way. Anyway, feel free to look around and see what I’m up to!
I have actually decided that I would like to encorporate space into what I like to do. I initially wanted to work with AI and neuroscience but now I want to also encorporate space into it. What I'm considering is to build things for the brain or to test how the brain develops in space and to hopefully allow humans to stay in space longer!
