About Me

Hey! I’m really interested in robotics, AI, and neuroscience, so I figured I’d make a website to share what I’m working on! I’ll be posting coding projects, things I’m learning, and whatever else I end up experimenting with along the way. I think it’s pretty cool how coding can connect to so many different things, especially how the brain works and how we can build machines that do things we never thought possible. I’m still figuring a lot of things out, so there will probably be some trial and error (and a lot of Googling). I made this site to keep track of my progress, share my projects on GitHub, and hopefully come up with some new ideas along the way. Who knows what I’ll end up building next? Anyway, feel free to look around and see what I’m up to!
